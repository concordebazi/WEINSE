WEINSE Program

Capacity Building

Strengthening women-led organisations, community groups and networks with the knowledge, skills and structures needed for sustainable growth and stronger impact.

About the Program

Stronger Organisations. Greater Impact.

WEINSE's Capacity Building program helps organisations and community groups strengthen their internal capabilities, leadership and long-term sustainability.

Through practical support, learning and organisational development, participants can improve their ability to plan, manage projects, mobilise resources, collaborate and create lasting impact.

Our Goal

To strengthen the knowledge, systems and leadership that enable women-led organisations and networks to become resilient, effective and sustainable.

What We Offer

Capacity Building Areas

Organisational Governance

Supporting organisations to strengthen leadership structures, responsibilities, decision-making and accountability.

Project Management

Building practical skills in project planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

Fundraising & Resource Mobilisation

Helping organisations identify funding opportunities, develop proposals and build sustainable resource strategies.

Leadership Development

Strengthening leadership skills, confidence, strategic thinking and effective organisational management.

Partnership Development

Supporting organisations to build meaningful partnerships, networks and collaborative initiatives.

Strategic Planning

Helping organisations define their priorities, goals, activities and long-term direction.

Who Is It For?

Building Stronger Community Organisations

The Capacity Building program is designed for organisations and groups that want to strengthen their structures, leadership, project delivery and ability to create sustainable social impact.

✓ Women's organisations
✓ Migrant organisations and networks
✓ Community-based organisations
✓ Emerging women leaders
✓ Non-profit and civil society organisations
