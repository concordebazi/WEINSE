WEINSE Program

Coaching & Mentoring

Supporting women to build confidence, strengthen their skills, develop their careers and create meaningful pathways toward personal and professional growth.

About the Program

Guidance, Support and Growth

WEINSE's Coaching and Mentoring program provides women with supportive guidance to help them identify their strengths, develop clear goals and increase their confidence.

Through individual and group-based mentoring, participants can strengthen their professional direction, leadership abilities, networks and personal development.

Our Focus

We create supportive spaces where women can learn, reflect, build confidence and receive guidance from mentors, professionals and peers.

What We Offer

Coaching & Mentoring Areas

Personal Development

Supporting women to understand their strengths, build self-confidence and develop personal goals.

Career Guidance

Helping participants explore career opportunities, professional pathways and strategies for entering or progressing in working life.

Leadership Development

Strengthening communication, decision-making, leadership and professional confidence.

Networking

Connecting women with mentors, professionals, organisations and networks that can support their development.

Goal Setting

Helping participants define realistic goals and develop practical steps toward achieving them.

Peer Mentoring

Creating opportunities for women to share knowledge, experiences and support one another.

Who Is It For?

Women at Different Stages of Their Journey

The program can support women who are entering the labour market, changing careers, developing leadership skills, building a business, expanding their networks or seeking greater confidence and direction.

✓ Women seeking career guidance
✓ Migrant and newly arrived women
✓ Women developing leadership skills
✓ Entrepreneurs and aspiring entrepreneurs
✓ Women seeking stronger professional networks
