Research

About the Program

Knowledge for Understanding and Change

WEINSE's Research program explores issues affecting women from diverse backgrounds, with particular attention to empowerment, migration, integration, equality and participation.

Through research, documentation and knowledge sharing, WEINSE aims to contribute to a better understanding of women's experiences, opportunities and barriers within communities and society.

Our Purpose

We use research and knowledge development to identify challenges, highlight women's experiences and support informed programs, policies and community initiatives.

Our Focus

Research Areas

Women & Integration

Exploring women's experiences of integration, participation and inclusion in their communities and wider society.

Gender Equality

Examining opportunities, inequalities and factors influencing women's participation and empowerment.

Employment & Economic Inclusion

Exploring barriers and opportunities affecting women's access to employment, entrepreneurship and economic independence.

Migration

Developing knowledge about migrant women's experiences, challenges, resources and pathways toward inclusion.

Social Participation

Examining women's participation in community life, organisations, networks and decision-making.

Community Development

Understanding how networks, organisations and community initiatives can strengthen women's participation and opportunities.

How We Work

Research Connected to Real Experiences

WEINSE seeks to connect research with the experiences of women, organisations and communities. This helps ensure that knowledge development remains relevant to real challenges and opportunities.

✓ Community-based research
✓ Surveys and data collection
✓ Interviews and focus groups
✓ Reports and knowledge sharing
✓ Collaboration with organisations and institutions

Collaborate With WEINSE

We welcome opportunities to collaborate with organisations, institutions and researchers on initiatives related to women empowerment and integration.
