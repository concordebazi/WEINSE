WEINSE Program

Training

Practical and inclusive learning opportunities designed to strengthen women's skills, employability, leadership, confidence and participation in society.

About the Program

Learning That Creates Opportunities

WEINSE provides training activities that equip women with practical knowledge, transferable skills and tools that can support personal, professional and organisational development.

Our training approach is designed to be accessible, interactive and relevant to the realities women face in employment, entrepreneurship, leadership and integration.

Our Approach

WEINSE combines practical learning, group participation, discussion and real-life examples to create meaningful learning experiences.

What We Offer

Training Areas

Employability Skills

Practical support in job searching, CV development, interview preparation and understanding the labour market.

Entrepreneurship

Building knowledge in business development, planning, innovation, financial awareness and sustainable entrepreneurship.

Digital Skills

Strengthening digital confidence and practical technology skills for work, education, communication and business.

Leadership

Developing leadership, communication, decision-making and confidence for women in professional and community roles.

Communication Skills

Supporting participants to communicate confidently, present ideas and build stronger professional relationships.

Integration & Participation

Building knowledge and confidence that support active participation in workplaces, organisations and communities.

Who Is It For?

Training for Women, Groups and Organisations

WEINSE training can support women at different stages of their personal and professional development, as well as organisations and community groups seeking stronger skills and knowledge.

✓ Women seeking employment
✓ Migrant and newly arrived women
✓ Entrepreneurs and aspiring entrepreneurs
✓ Women in leadership and community roles
✓ Women's organisations and community groups
