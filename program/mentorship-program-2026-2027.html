WEINSE • 2026–2027

Mentorship Programme 2026–2027

By Women Empowerment and Integration Network (WEINSE)

We Want You to Be Part of It!

Grow, Inspire and Empower Other Women

Would you like to grow, inspire, and empower other women while developing yourself?

Welcome to WEINSE’s Mentorship Programme, where we create opportunities for migrant women to share experiences, exchange knowledge, and build valuable networks.

As a mentor, you will have the opportunity to share your knowledge, experiences, and lessons learned to support other women in their personal and professional development. As a mentee, you will receive support, gain new perspectives, and have the opportunity to discuss challenges with someone who can offer guidance and inspiration.

We believe in the power of women supporting women. Through mentorship, we can build confidence, create new opportunities, and promote greater participation and integration into society. Together, we build bridges between experiences, cultures, and networks.

WEINSE’s Mentorship Programme launched in September, offering an opportunity to learn, grow, and develop together.

Whether you want to share your experience or receive support on your own journey, there is a place for you with us.

Become part of WEINSE and help make a difference for yourself and for other women!

Programme Details

When?

The programme runs from 2 September 2026 to 28 February 2027.

Where?

Meetings can take place in person or online, depending on the preferences of each mentor–mentee pair.

How often?

Meetings take place once a month, or more frequently if the mentor–mentee pair wishes. In addition to these meetings, we will organise a joint kick-off event, a closing event, and possibly an opportunity to meet in person with everyone participating in the mentorship programme.

We expect all meetings, except for one, to be held online.

Who Can Participate?

Anyone who is, or becomes, a member of WEINSE can apply. We will do our best to match as many mentor–mentee pairs as possible, but we cannot guarantee that everyone who applies will be matched with a mentor or mentee.

Mentor or Mentee?

If you have experience that you believe could benefit others, we encourage you to apply as a mentor, regardless of your age or how many years you have worked in your field.

If you would like to focus on your own challenges and personal development, you can apply as a mentee.

You can also apply for both roles if you would like to be matched with two people during the programme — one as a mentor and one as a mentee.

How Do I Apply?

The registration deadline for the 2026–2027 programme was 31 August 2026.

2026–2027 Application for the WEINSE Mentorship Programme.

Do you have any questions, or would you like to know more? Feel free to contact us by email at info@weinse.se . We would be happy to tell you more!

More About the Programme

The programme started in September and runs until the end of February. During this period, each mentor–mentee pair meets regularly, preferably several times a month, either in person or online.

In addition, we will organise joint gatherings to provide inspiration, encourage networking, and facilitate the exchange of experiences.

  • Programme duration: Approximately six months.
  • Mentor–mentee meetings: Held online or in person, with one session per month involving all participants in the mentorship programme.
  • Participation: Free of charge.
