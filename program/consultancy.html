WEINSE Program

Consultancy

Professional advice, strategic support and practical guidance for organisations working with women empowerment, migration, diversity, inclusion and integration.

About the Program

Knowledge That Supports Better Decisions

WEINSE provides consultancy services to organisations, institutions and community actors that want to strengthen their work related to women, gender equality, integration and social participation.

Our consultancy approach combines practical experience, research, dialogue and an understanding of the challenges and opportunities faced by women from diverse backgrounds.

Our Approach

We work collaboratively with organisations to identify needs, develop solutions and strengthen strategies that create more inclusive and sustainable outcomes.

What We Offer

Consultancy Areas

Gender Equality

Supporting organisations to develop more inclusive approaches, policies and practices that respond to women's needs and experiences.

Integration Strategies

Advice and support for initiatives that improve participation, inclusion and integration for migrant and newly arrived women.

Organisational Development

Supporting organisations to strengthen structures, processes, planning and ways of working.

Program Design

Guidance in developing programs, activities and interventions that respond to community needs and create measurable impact.

Diversity & Inclusion

Helping organisations improve diversity, inclusion, participation and representation across their work.

Stakeholder Engagement

Supporting stronger collaboration between organisations, communities, institutions and women-led networks.

Who Is It For?

Supporting Organisations and Institutions

WEINSE consultancy services are designed for organisations and institutions seeking practical support in strengthening their work with women, migration, inclusion, integration and community participation.

✓ Non-governmental organisations
✓ Municipalities and public institutions
✓ Women's organisations and networks
✓ Community-based organisations
✓ Projects and development initiatives
