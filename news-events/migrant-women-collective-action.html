About the Event

Leading Change Through Collective Action

This forum brings migrant women and organisations together to explore how leadership and collective action can contribute to positive change.

The event provides a space for participants to share experiences, exchange ideas and identify opportunities to work together on issues that affect migrant women and their communities.

Through stronger networks, collaboration and collective action, migrant women can strengthen their voices, increase their participation and contribute to lasting change in society.

Why This Forum?

Collective action can help migrant women strengthen their voices, build solidarity and work together to address shared challenges. By connecting women and organisations, the forum supports stronger networks and more coordinated action for positive change.

Collective Leadership

Strengthen migrant women’s ability to lead together, share responsibility and create positive change through collective leadership.

Collective Action

Encourage migrant women and organisations to work together, identify shared priorities and take coordinated action to address common challenges.

Solidarity & Networking

Build stronger relationships between migrant women, organisations and community actors to strengthen solidarity, share experiences and support one another.

Voice & Influence

Strengthen migrant women’s voices and influence by creating opportunities to participate in dialogue, raise shared concerns and contribute to decisions that affect their communities.

Community Mobilisation

Encourage migrant women and organisations to mobilise around shared priorities, engage their communities and work together to create meaningful and sustainable change.

Partnership & Cooperation

Promote stronger cooperation between migrant women, organisations, networks and community actors to develop partnerships and support coordinated action for lasting positive change.

Building Networks

Develop lasting relationships between migrant organisations and other community stakeholders.

Who Can Participate?

A Forum for Collective Action

The forum welcomes migrant women, organisations and community actors interested in working together to strengthen women's leadership, solidarity, participation and collective action.

✓ Migrant women
✓ Migrant women-led organisations
✓ Women’s networks and community groups
✓ Civil society organisations
✓ Community actors interested in collective action
