About the Event

Migrant Women Leading Change

This forum brings together migrant women and migrant women-led organisations to strengthen leadership, participation and collaboration.

The event creates a space for women to exchange experiences, share knowledge and discuss how migrant women can play an active role in creating positive change in their organisations and communities.

With a focus on democratic participation, the forum encourages migrant women to strengthen their voices, build networks and participate more actively in decision-making processes and society.

Why This Forum?

Migrant women play an important role in their organisations and communities. Strengthening leadership, networks and democratic participation can help create greater opportunities for women to influence decisions, contribute to society and lead positive change.

Forum Focus

Connect. Exchange. Collaborate.

The forum creates a space for meaningful conversations and stronger networks.

Women’s Leadership

Strengthen the leadership skills, confidence and capacity of migrant women to lead change in their organisations and communities.

Democratic Participation

Encourage migrant women to participate actively in democratic processes, community dialogue and decision-making at different levels.

Networking & Collaboration

Build stronger connections between migrant women, women-led organisations and community actors to exchange experiences and develop opportunities for collaboration.

Women Leading Change

Empower migrant women to use their knowledge, experience and leadership to create positive change in their organisations, communities and wider society.

Voice & Representation

Strengthen migrant women's voices and representation by creating opportunities to express their perspectives, participate in public dialogue and contribute to decisions that affect their communities.

Building Networks

Develop lasting relationships between migrant organisations and other community stakeholders.

Who Can Participate?

A Forum for Leadership and Participation

The forum welcomes migrant women and organisations interested in strengthening women's leadership, democratic participation, representation and community engagement.
