About the Event

Connecting Women and Organisations

The Women Migrant Organisation Forum and Network brings together women, migrant organisations, community actors and networks in Gothenburg.

The forum provides an opportunity to meet, exchange experiences, build relationships and explore opportunities for stronger collaboration.

Through dialogue and networking, WEINSE aims to strengthen connections between organisations and support greater participation and empowerment of migrant women.

Why This Forum?

Strong networks can help organisations exchange knowledge, develop partnerships and create new opportunities for women and their communities.

Forum Focus

Connect. Exchange. Collaborate.

The forum creates a space for meaningful conversations and stronger networks.

Networking

Connect with women, organisations, community actors and other networks.

Knowledge Exchange

Share experiences, perspectives, ideas and lessons from different organisations and communities.

Collaboration

Explore opportunities for partnerships, joint initiatives and stronger organisational cooperation.

Women's Empowerment

Discuss approaches that strengthen women's confidence, participation and opportunities.

Integration

Exchange perspectives on participation, inclusion and integration of migrant women in society.

Building Networks

Develop lasting relationships between migrant organisations and other community stakeholders.
