About WEINSE

Empowering Women. Strengthening Communities.

Women Empowerment and Integration Network (WEINSE) works to strengthen women's participation, opportunities and inclusion through empowerment, capacity building, advocacy, learning and strong networks.

Who We Are

A Network for Empowerment and Integration

Women Empowerment and Integration Network (WEINSE) is an independent organisation committed to promoting the empowerment of women and girls.

Our work focuses on capacity building, advocacy, coaching, mentoring, consultancy and research.

WEINSE creates spaces where women can strengthen their skills, increase their confidence, build meaningful networks and participate more actively in society.

Empower. Integrate. Inspire.

Our approach combines empowerment, knowledge, participation and collaboration to create sustainable opportunities for women and communities.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower women and girls by strengthening their skills, confidence, networks and opportunities, while supporting greater inclusion, integration and participation in society.

🌍

Our Vision

A society where every woman has equal opportunities, access to resources and the confidence to participate, lead, contribute and succeed.

What Guides Us

Our Values

The values that guide WEINSE's work and relationships.

💜

Empowerment

We support women to recognise their strengths, develop their abilities and create opportunities for themselves and others.

🤝

Inclusion

We promote participation, belonging and equal opportunities for women from diverse backgrounds.

🌱

Growth

We believe in continuous learning, personal development and stronger organisations and communities.

🌍

Diversity

We value different experiences, perspectives, cultures and knowledge as sources of strength.

💡

Knowledge

We use learning, research and shared experience to support better decisions and sustainable change.

🫶

Collaboration
