About WEINSE

Empowering Women. Strengthening Communities.

Women Empowerment Integration Network (WEINSE) focuses on exclusive women’s capacity building and advocacy. WEINSE is an independent international organization committed to women’s and girls’ empowerment by engaging in research, coaching, mentoring, and consultancies

By making visible the persistent gender gap in financial inclusion, acknowledging the differences in treatment between women and men, and the different ways by which they are impacted by regulations and laws, we might finally start to change the system to fit women’s needs. What we need are not supposedly “neutral” policies that harm women, but the recognition of women’s needs as just as important as men’s.
